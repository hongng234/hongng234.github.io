SATELLITE OPERATIONS / OPTIMIZATION · OCT 2026

Satellite task scheduler

Python earth-observation scheduler with battery, memory, slew-time and per-orbit constraints, comparing a CP-SAT model with two greedy heuristics on 250–66k opportunities. CP-SAT was optimal up to 2k opportunities in under 0.5 s and served 100% of requests vs 98% for greedy. When CP-SAT stalled at 66k, I diagnosed it and added a greedy warm-start hint, explored with Claude Code — the result is never worse than greedy.

EARTH OBSERVATION / FORECASTING · OCT 2026

Multi-location flood risk prediction

Leakage-safe next-day flood-risk forecasting for 5 Vietnamese river locations from 5 years of daily rainfall (Open-Meteo) and GloFAS river discharge (~9k location-days). Benchmarked Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting against persistence and prior baselines with date-blocked time-series CV — best PR-AUC 0.89 on the 2024 hold-out vs 0.64 for persistence. Planned, reviewed and documented with Claude Code.

EARTH OBSERVATION / SAR · NOV 2026

SAR flood mapping

Built a pipeline that maps flooded areas from real Sentinel-1 radar satellite imagery, comparing classical thresholding, Random Forest and a U-Net. Doubled the IoU of the standard thresholding method (0.33 → 0.68), and on the Bolivia flood test the U-Net reached 0.64 vs 0.59 for global Otsu thresholding.
